Aboki Rate is published by Ismail Idris ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the Aboki Rate mobile application collects, why, and what you can do about it. It applies to the Android application with the package name ng.abokirate.app.

Aboki Rate reports Nigerian foreign-exchange rates. We do not buy or sell currency, do not match buyers with sellers, and never hold your money. The app is built so that it needs almost nothing from you, and this policy is short because there is little to describe.

What we do not collect

We want to be specific about this, because it is the larger part of the truth:

  • No name, email address, phone number or date of birth. The app never asks you to register or sign in.
  • No location. The app requests no location permission, of any precision.
  • No contacts, photos, files, camera or microphone access.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting service. There is no Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Sentry, Amplitude, Mixpanel, or Meta SDK in this application.
  • No advertising identifier used to profile you. See "Advertising" below.
  • No payment details. If you buy the ad-free upgrade, Google Play processes it. We never see your card.

What we do collect

An anonymous account

When you first open Aboki Rate, the app creates an anonymous account on our behalf so that any rate alerts you set are yours and cannot be read or changed by anyone else. That account is a random identifier. It is not linked to a name, an email address, a phone number, or any Google or social account.

If you never set a rate alert, that anonymous account is deleted automatically after 30 days of inactivity.

Rate alerts, if you create one

If you ask to be notified when a rate crosses a figure you choose, we store:

  • the currency and market you chose
  • which figure you are watching (buy, sell, or central), and whether you want to be told when it goes above or below
  • the figure you chose
  • a push notification token issued to your device

The push token is an address for your device, issued by Google, that lets us deliver a notification. It does not identify you personally and it changes if you reinstall the app. It is used for one purpose: sending the alert you asked for. It is never used for marketing and never shared with advertisers.

We also record whether each notification was accepted for delivery, so that a token belonging to an uninstalled app can be retired rather than written to forever.

You can delete an alert at any time in the app. Deleting it removes the stored figure and token for that alert.

Information stored only on your device

Some things never leave your phone: recently viewed rates cached so the app works offline, and a flag recording that you bought the ad-free upgrade. Uninstalling the app erases both.

Technical information handled by our suppliers

Our database provider (Supabase) records ordinary server logs, including the IP address making a request, as any web service does. These are used to operate and secure the service and are not used to build a profile of you.

Advertising

The free version of Aboki Rate shows banner advertisements supplied by Google AdMob.

We request non-personalised advertisements only. That means Google is instructed not to use a profile of your interests or past browsing to choose which advertisement you see. Advertisements are chosen from the content of the screen and your general location at country level.

Even for non-personalised advertisements, Google still processes limited data to serve the advertisement, measure it, and detect fraud — including your device's advertising identifier, IP address, and the fact that an advertisement was shown. Google's own privacy policy governs that processing: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543.

You can reset or delete your advertising identifier at any time in Settings → Privacy → Ads on your Android device.

We block advertising categories that would be inappropriate beside exchange-rate information, including cryptocurrency, loans and short-term credit, foreign-exchange trading, and get-rich-quick offers. Advertisement filtering is not perfect; if you see something that should not be there, please tell us and we will block it.

Removing advertisements

You can buy a one-time upgrade that removes all banner advertisements. Google Play handles the payment and tells the app that the purchase exists. We do not receive or store your payment details, and we do not keep a record of your purchase on our servers — the entitlement lives on your device and is restored through your Google account if you reinstall.

Promoted business posts are not advertisements sold by an advertising network. They are short notices written and paid for by Nigerian businesses, clearly labelled, and they remain visible after the ad-free upgrade. Buying the upgrade removes banner advertising; the app says so before you buy.

News

Aboki Rate links to foreign-exchange news published by other organisations. Opening an article does not report your reading back to us, and we do not build a history of what you read.

Business posts

Some screens carry short notices paid for by Nigerian businesses. Each one shows the business's own name and WhatsApp number, supplied by that business for the purpose of being contacted.

Seeing a post reports nothing about you to anyone. If you tap the contact button, the app opens WhatsApp with that business's number filled in — nothing is sent until you send it, and what happens in that conversation is between you and the business, governed by WhatsApp's privacy policy rather than ours.

We do not record which posts you viewed or contacted.

Who we share information with

We do not sell your information, and we have no advertising or marketing partners of our own.

We rely on three service providers to run the app:

Google (AdMob, Play, Firebase Cloud Messaging) Advertisements, purchases, notification delivery

We disclose information otherwise only where the law requires it.

Where information is held

Our database and functions run on servers outside Nigeria, and our service providers are based outside Nigeria. By using the app you accept that the limited information described above is processed in those locations.

How long we keep information

  • Anonymous accounts with no alerts: deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Rate alerts: kept until you delete them, or until you uninstall the app and the account is purged.
  • Push tokens: retired as soon as we learn a device no longer accepts notifications.
  • Rate observations and news: kept indefinitely. These are records of public market information and contain nothing about you.

Children

Aboki Rate is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Advertisements are requested at Google's strictest content rating.

Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, and under the GDPR if you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you may ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it.

In practice, the fastest route is in the app: delete your alerts, or uninstall it, and the information described above goes with it. If you would rather we did it, write to us at the address below. Because accounts are anonymous, we may need you to tell us something that lets us find the record — for example the alert you set and roughly when.

You can also turn notifications off entirely in your device settings at any time.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date at the top and, where the change affects what we collect, say so in the app.
